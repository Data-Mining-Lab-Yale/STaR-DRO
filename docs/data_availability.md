# Data availability

We evaluate STaR-DRO on EPPC Miner, a corpus of patient–provider secure messages from Yale New Haven Health. The study comprises 1,200 messages, split at message level into 840 training, 180 validation, and 180 test messages. The task predicts hierarchical annotations with nine Codes and 38 Sub-codes, together with supporting evidence Spans.

As stated in the manuscript, **the datasets generated and analyzed during our study are not publicly available because access to data derived from patient–provider secure messages is restricted by the IRB to protect patient privacy**. Data access and use follow the study's institutional governance and privacy safeguards.

Researchers can apply STaR-DRO to their own authorized data using the [prompt templates](../prompts/structured_prediction.md) and [step-by-step preparation workflow](data_format.md#step-by-step-dataset-preparation).
